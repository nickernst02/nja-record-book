Why old scores look small

League scoring per team per game

One line, 2005 through 2025. Worth knowing before anyone brags about a number from a different decade.

2005 teams averaged 69.9 a game. 2024 averaged 119.0. That is almost entirely scoring-system changes, not seventeen years of collective self-improvement. The settings have been reworked repeatedly, most visibly in the jump from 2022 to 2023. A 2006 blowout and a 2024 blowout are not the same event, and nothing in the record book compares raw scores across eras.
